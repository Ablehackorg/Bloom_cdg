Bloom is a platform dedicated to supporting independent filmmakers and creators who tell stories that matter.

We believe in cinema’s power to inspire change and bring communities together. Our mission is to provide resources, funding, and mentorship for visionary creators aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Through our network of investors, volunteers, and industry professionals, we’ve helped launch 50+ impact projects across 30+ countries.
